WakeUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short

WakeUp is an alarm clock that works completely on your phone. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads and no analytics or tracking
  • No internet permission, so the app cannot send data anywhere
  • Everything you set up stays on your device and disappears when you uninstall the app

Who we are

WakeUp is published by Pax (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at pax.official.com@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

To work as an alarm clock, WakeUp keeps the following in the app’s private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device.

  • Alarms: time, label, repeat days, sound, vibration and snooze choices.
  • App settings: theme, 12/24-hour clock and default values for new alarms.
  • Stopwatch: the elapsed time and lap times, so it can keep running when you leave the screen.
  • Your own alarm sounds: copies of audio files you choose to add (see below).

We do not have access to this information. WakeUp does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or any other files on your device.

Your own alarm sounds

If you add your own audio file, WakeUp makes a copy of it in its private storage so it can play it as an alarm. The original file is not changed. The copy is not uploaded or shared. You can delete it at any time from the sound chooser inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes every copy.

If you choose one of your phone’s own alarm sounds instead, WakeUp only remembers which sound you picked.

Permissions and why WakeUp asks for them

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShows the ringing alarm with its Stop and Snooze buttons, and the “snoozed” and “missed alarm” notices.
Exact alarmsMakes the alarm ring at the exact minute you chose.
Full-screen notificationsShows the alarm screen over the lock screen when it rings.
Run at startupRestores your alarms after the phone restarts.
Vibrate and wake lockVibrates and keeps the phone awake while an alarm is ringing.
Foreground service (media playback)Keeps the alarm sound playing reliably until you stop it. It runs only while an alarm is ringing.

WakeUp does not request the internet permission.

Third parties

WakeUp contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking code, and it does not share anything with third parties. Android’s cloud backup is switched off for the app, so alarm data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

Two actions open another app on your phone, only when you tap them: the privacy policy link opens your web browser (which has its own privacy practices), and the contact address opens your email app.

Your control and deleting your data

Because all information stays on your phone, you are in control of it. Delete an alarm or a custom sound inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling WakeUp.

Children

WakeUp does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
