## Summary

WakeUp is an alarm clock that works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## Information the app handles

- **Alarm settings** (times, labels, repeat days, sound, vibration and snooze choices) and **app preferences** (theme,
  time format, defaults). These are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent
  anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- WakeUp does not ask for an account, does not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone, and contains no
  advertising or analytics code.
- WakeUp does not request the internet permission and cannot send data over the network. (The privacy policy link
  opens in your browser, which is a separate app.)

## Permissions and why they are used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Show the ringing alarm and the Stop / Snooze buttons, and the "snoozed" and "missed alarm" notices |
| Exact alarms | Ring at the exact time you chose |
| Full-screen notifications | Show the alarm screen over the lock screen |
| Run at startup | Restore your alarms after the phone restarts |
| Vibrate / Wake lock | Vibrate and keep the phone awake while an alarm rings |
| Foreground service (media playback) | Keep the alarm sound playing reliably until you stop it |

## Alarm sounds

When you choose a sound, WakeUp stores only a reference to the sound that Android's own sound picker returns. It does not copy or upload the file.

## Children

WakeUp does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
